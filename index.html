01 / The experience

Small details.
Big difference.

A calm, personal space for discovering, organising, rating, and keeping track of movies and TV series — with useful improvements being built around it.

FEATURE / 01⤴

Multi-API fallback

Planned support for fallback data sources, helping title discovery stay more resilient when an API is unavailable.

FEATURE / 02⌁

Local peer-to-peer sync

Explore local Wi-Fi device-to-device syncing, with the project’s sync capability marked as beta.

FEATURE / 03▷

Trailer playback

Keep previews close to the browsing experience with an in-built trailer player.

FEATURE / 04▥

Personal library, your way

Build a watchlist, mark titles watched, rate movies and episodes, add notes, and organise your own custom lists.

FEATURE / 05◴

Useful analytics

A planned analytics page to make your media activity easier to understand at a glance.

FEATURE / 06↻

Automatic backups

A planned backup flow with a user-chosen folder, so your setup can stay organised.

02 / Open by design

Curious minds are welcome.

Ehsaan Play is designed around a simple idea: your personal library belongs to you. Its repository is public, and the app can keep library data locally in your browser without requiring a traditional account or personal cloud database.
